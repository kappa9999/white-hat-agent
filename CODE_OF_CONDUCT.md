# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a harassment-free, technically rigorous community. Contributors should be respectful, critique
claims with evidence, credit sources and prior work, protect confidential reports, and make the project accessible to
people across languages, backgrounds, and levels of AI experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, deliberate exposure of secrets
or undisclosed vulnerabilities, plagiarism, fabricated evidence, and using project spaces to coordinate attacks on
targets without authorization.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the repository's private contact channel. The sole
maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation.
Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, recurrence, and risk to affected people or systems.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review threads, community spaces, and when someone is
officially representing the project. It does not attempt to govern independent research or speech outside project
spaces.
